Assumptions, constraints, and risks

What this solution assumes, and what it refuses

The repository is a working studio and a deployable Container App. Several boundaries are deliberate. Treating them as already solved will mis-state the product.

One replica

A second copy would not see this match, this studio, or this encode.

StatsBomb off

Open Data is non-commercial and must not ship in a paid offer.

No purchase flow

Marketplace fulfillment is not in this repository.

Speech on air

Only Azure speech is captured. The browser voice is silence.

Assumptions

  • The operator who deploys has an Azure CLI session and permission to create the resource group, the Entra application, and the role assignments.
  • The container image can run ffmpeg and a Chromium browser. The studio’s own camera path needs a camera on that machine; a server uses guests instead.
  • Clubs and players in the synthetic match are fictional. A head-to-head is the matches this installation has finished, at most the newest 50.
  • Viewers of one match share narration. Personalization is a filter at the gateway (mode, language, favourite), not a separate model call per viewer.
  • A model endpoint is optional. Templates are the supported path when Foundry and KICKSTREAM_LLM_* are unset.

Constraints

StatsBomb Open Data is non-commercial and must not ship in a paid offer. A deploy leaves real sources off unless real_sources is set true. Attribution (logo and “Data: StatsBomb”) is required wherever that source is used.

Kickstream does not implement Microsoft Marketplace SaaS fulfillment, an Entra purchase landing page, or a live Partner Center offer. Publishing and co-sell are outside this repository.

  • min_replicas = 1 and max_replicas = 1. A second replica would not share the in-memory match, studio, or ffmpeg process.
  • Live API-Football and OpenLigaDB feeds have no ball positions. Widgets that need a pitch coordinate say so, rather than inventing one.
  • API-Football’s free plan is about 100 requests a day. One match costs on the order of 80, including fixture lists. The budget file follows the provider’s count.
  • OpenLigaDB is community-entered. Goals can arrive late. The minute and half-time are estimated.
  • Explainability prose and Ask Copilot answers are English. Narration for the program can be English, Spanish, or Hindi, in Fan or Analyst wording.
  • The stream has no bed music and no crowd. Silence means nobody unmuted is in the call, or auto live fell back to a browser voice the capture cannot hear.
  • Highlight times are fixed: a reel starts 8 seconds before the moment and runs 15 seconds. The model may choose the moment and the words. It does not choose the edit points.
  • Container Registry admin credentials and the storage account key are how the current templates pull the image and mount /data.

Risks

RiskWhat actually happens
Replica restartMemory of the live match and the ffmpeg process is gone. Files on the share and the Cosmos match log remain. Auto live restarts a 24/7 stream from saved settings. A studio show does not resume mid-encode by itself.
Public viewWith the default public_view, anyone can watch /live and /match read-only. Studio routes still require a studio user.
Empty public-host listA tunnel opened while KICKSTREAM_PUBLIC_HOSTS is empty exposes the whole studio, including stream controls.
Co-hostA co-host can end or delete the broadcast and can go live. Treat the invite as an operator credential.
Speech spendS0 bills per character. The daily cap stops Azure speech; it does not stop the stream from going quiet when the browser voice cannot be captured.
DiskRecord to file combined with Stream 24/7 fills the 10 GiB share. The product’s own guidance is to stream 24/7 to platforms only.
Registry admin userThe admin password sits in a Container App secret. Rotating it means updating that secret and the registry together.
Untested live adaptersThe X and Tavily adapters are checked against documented payloads. They have not been run against those services in this repository’s test suite.
One destination failsThe others continue. The top bar shows the failed one. There is no automatic failover to a second region: Cosmos has a single geo location.

Taken together, the safe commercial reading is: operate the synthetic match, or a feed you have a right to use; keep StatsBomb off any paid offer; and do not describe a Marketplace purchase flow as part of the product that ships today.
